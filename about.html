Social_auto

Social_auto is a personal tool built and used by a single creator (the owner of this page) to manage his own social media content.

What it does

The owner connects his own TikTok account once through TikTok Login Kit. After that, videos that the owner has created himself are uploaded to the drafts inbox of that same account through the TikTok Content Posting API (Upload to TikTok). The owner then opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, edits it if needed and publishes it manually. The tool never publishes content on its own.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not access any other person's TikTok account.

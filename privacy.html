Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

What data is used

Social_auto is used by one person (the owner) with his own TikTok account. When the owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives an access token and a refresh token for that account (scopes: user.info.basic and video.upload). The tokens are used only to upload the owner's own videos to the drafts inbox of that account.

Storage and sharing

The tokens are stored in a private, access-controlled database that only the owner can reach. They are never shown publicly, sold, or shared with any third party. The tool does not collect, store or process personal data of any other TikTok user.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the tool. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app settings and delete the stored tokens, which ends all access.
